# singapore-hdb-market-analytics

**Project**

Singapore HDB Resale Market Intelligence & Opportunity Analysis

**Business scenario**

Assume you’re supporting a Singapore property marketplace/property-services company.

**Business objective**

Identify HDB resale market trends, understand factors associated with transaction prices, and identify towns and property segments that may warrant greater commercial attention based on market size, growth and transaction activity.

**Main decision**

Which HDB resale market segments should the business prioritize?
